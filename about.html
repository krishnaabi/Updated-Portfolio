My story.

I started my career in tech and gradually found my way into design — first through visual design, then UI/UX and product design.

Over the last 4.5+ years, I've worked across branding, digital experiences and product interfaces, learning how design decisions affect both people and businesses.

Today, I work across the full design process — understanding the problem, shaping the experience, designing the interface and turning ideas into something people can actually use.

✦ CORE PRINCIPLES ✦

What I believe in.

Design is not just what it looks like.
It's how it works, how it makes people feel,
and the impact it creates.

01

Start with
the problem

Before designing the screen, I want to understand what we're actually trying to solve.

→
02

Make complexity
feel simple

Good design doesn't show how complicated the system is. It makes the experience feel obvious.

03

Design for
real people

Decisions should come from user behaviour, context and needs — not just assumptions.

04

Details create
trust

Small interactions, spacing, hierarchy and consistency can completely change how a product feels.

05

Design with
the team

The best work happens when design, business and technology move in the same direction.
